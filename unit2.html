Using Stepper Motors with Arduino

Objective:

The goal of this stage of my innovation project was to connect a NEMA 17 stepper motor to an Arduino and create a working prototype that could control the motor's rotation. I wanted to understand how hardware components, electrical connections, and software work together to produce a physical movement. I also wanted to build a foundation that I could use in future projects.

Process:

Youtube Tutorial Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wcLeXXATCR4&t=657s

1. Research: I researched my YEJMKJ NEMA 17 stepper motor and learned which components I needed to connect it to an Arduino. 2. Gather materials: I gathered my Arduino Uno, A4988 motor driver, breadboard, jumper wires, and external power supply. 3. Connect the driver: I placed the A4988 driver on the breadboard and connected its DIR and STEP pins to Arduino pins 2 and 3. 4. Connect the motor: I connected the motor’s four wires to the driver’s output pins, making sure the wires were paired correctly. 5. Connect the power: I connected the required power and ground wires and checked the connections before turning on the circuit. 6. Write the code: I wrote code in the Arduino IDE to send signals to the driver and control the motor’s movement and direction. 7. Test the motor: I uploaded the code and tested the setup to see if the motor moved as expected.

Challenges:

In my opinion, the biggest challenge was figuring out the wiring, as it was very different and more difficult than what we had been exploring in earlier classes. One of the main things I learned was that the motor needs a driver instead of being connected directly to the Arduino. I had to understand what the driver does and why the motor needs a separate power supply. I also learned that connecting the motor wires correctly is important. If the wires are connected incorrectly, the motor may not rotate as expected. This taught me to check the wiring, read the component instructions, and look for possible causes of problems instead of changing connections randomly.
